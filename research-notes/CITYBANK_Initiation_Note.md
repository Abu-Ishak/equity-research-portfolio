## 2. Investment Thesis Highlights

1. Undisputed market leader in Bangladesh credit cards (American Express) and Citytouch digital banking.
2. Trading at just 0.73x P/B (BDT 30.2 vs NAV BDT 41.51) despite ~19.5% ROE and AAA/ST-1 rating.
3. Asset quality tightened sharply (FY25 NPL 2.50% vs 3.72% FY24; Q3 2026: 2.79%) with 16.05% CRAR.
4. Fee-income and retail card float insulate spreads across shifting Bangladesh Bank policy rate cycles.
